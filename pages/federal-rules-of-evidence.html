The Federal Rules of Evidence govern what a jury sees, hears, and ultimately believes. They are the invisible architecture of every federal trial — the rules that decide whether a dying declaration is heard, whether a prior conviction destroys a witness, whether a business record speaks for itself.

This guide was written for practicing attorneys, law clerks, judicial staff, and advanced law students who need a sharp, practical command of the FRE — not a textbook recitation, but a working reference that explains why each rule exists and how skilled advocates use it in real courtrooms.
